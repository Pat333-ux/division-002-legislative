Title
Recognition and Establishment of the Judicial Division

Date Issued
October 22, 2026

Status
Active

Matter Before The Division
Review and recognition of the establishment, authority, responsibilities, and governance function of Division 003 Judicial within the New World Order DAO ecosystem.

Findings of Fact
The New World Order DAO has established multiple governance divisions to support organizational operations.

A need exists for an independent review authority responsible for governance interpretation, compliance oversight, dispute resolution, and appeals review.

The Judicial Division has adopted governing documents including:

Judicial Charter
Judicial Constitution
Judicial Procedures
Code of Judicial Ethics
Appeals Framework
Compliance Review Framework
The establishment of a dedicated Judicial Division promotes transparency, accountability, consistency, and governance continuity.
Analysis
The creation of an independent Judicial Division supports organizational stability through structured review processes, documented rulings, formal opinions, compliance oversight, and preservation of governance records.

A review authority strengthens confidence in governance operations by providing an established mechanism for appeals, policy interpretation, and resolution of organizational disputes.

Determination
The Judicial Division is hereby formally recognized as the independent governance review authority of the New World Order DAO.

The Judicial Division possesses authority to:

Review governance actions
Publish judicial opinions
Issue rulings
Conduct compliance reviews
Evaluate appeals
Interpret governance standards
Maintain judicial records
Orders
Division 003 Judicial shall maintain official governance records.

Judicial opinions and rulings shall be documented within the Judicial repository.

Compliance reviews may be conducted as authorized by governing standards.

Appeals may be accepted and processed according to established Judicial procedures.

Future governance reviews shall utilize published Judicial standards and frameworks.

Effective Date
This ruling shall become effective immediately upon publication.

Appeals Framework
Division 003 Judicial
Purpose
The Appeals Framework establishes the official process for requesting review of administrative, executive, legislative, and governance decisions within the New World Order DAO.

Section 1 - Right to Appeal
Any member, division, officer, or authorized participant may submit an appeal concerning:

Administrative decisions
Executive actions
Legislative directives
Compliance findings
Governance determinations
Judicial rulings permitted for review
Section 2 - Filing Requirements
An appeal shall contain:

Appeal Number
Date Submitted
Appellant
Matter Under Review
Statement of Concern
Requested Remedy
Supporting Documentation
Incomplete appeals may be returned for correction.

Section 3 - Grounds for Appeal
Appeals may be accepted based upon:

New evidence
Procedural concerns
Misapplication of policy
Governance interpretation disputes
Compliance concerns
Administrative error
Section 4 - Appeal Intake
Upon receipt, the Judicial Division shall:

Assign a case number
Verify jurisdiction
Review available documentation
Determine review eligibility
Open an appeal record
Section 5 - Review Process
The review process may include:

Policy analysis
Evidence evaluation
Compliance examination
Governance review
Prior ruling comparison
All findings shall be documented.

Section 6 - Determinations
Appeals may result in:

Affirmation
Modification
Reversal
Remand for further review
Dismissal
Section 7 - Written Decisions
All final appeal decisions shall include:

Case Number
Findings of Fact
Applicable Governance Standards
Determination
Effective Date
Section 8 - Record Preservation
Appeal records shall be maintained within Judicial archives for governance continuity and future reference.

Section 9 - Transparency
Final decisions may be published when appropriate to support accountability and organizational learning.

Section 10 - Organizational Wellbeing
Appeal reviews shall consider fairness, transparency, due process, governance integrity, and the long-term wellbeing of the organization and its members.

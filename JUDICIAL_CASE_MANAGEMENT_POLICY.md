Judicial Case Management Policy
Division 003 Judicial
Purpose
This policy establishes the official system for creating, numbering, tracking, monitoring, maintaining, and archiving judicial matters reviewed by the Judicial Division.

Section 1 - Case Registration
Every judicial matter shall receive a unique case identifier upon acceptance.

Case types include:

Appeals
Compliance Reviews
Judicial Opinions
Governance Reviews
Policy Interpretations
Administrative Reviews
Section 2 - Case Numbering Format
Case numbering standard:

JD-YYYY-XXXX

Example:

JD-2026-0001

Where:

JD = Judicial Division
YYYY = Year
XXXX = Sequential Case Number
Section 3 - Case Categories
Cases shall be classified according to subject matter.

Categories include:

Governance
Appeals
Compliance
Operations
Administrative
Constitutional
Legislative Review
Executive Review
Section 4 - Case Statuses
Cases shall maintain one of the following statuses:

Submitted
Case received and awaiting review.

Accepted
Case accepted within judicial jurisdiction.

Under Review
Case actively being examined.

Investigation
Additional information gathering is underway.

Decision Pending
Review has concluded and determination is being prepared.

Decided
Ruling or opinion has been issued.

Closed
Case has reached final disposition.

Archived
Case preserved for permanent recordkeeping.

Section 5 - Case Records
Each case file shall include:

Case Number
Filing Date
Subject
Parties
Evidence
Findings
Determinations
Final Outcome
Section 6 - Documentation Standards
All decisions, findings, recommendations, and communications shall be documented within the official case record.

Section 7 - Retention Requirements
Case records shall be preserved to maintain:

Governance continuity
Institutional knowledge
Historical reference
Compliance accountability
Section 8 - Access Controls
Public, internal, and confidential designations may be assigned according to the nature of the matter under review.

Section 9 - Archive Management
Closed cases shall be transferred to permanent archives and retained according to Judicial Division record preservation requirements.

Section 10 - Reporting
The Judicial Division may publish periodic summaries regarding:

Case volume
Categories reviewed
Compliance trends
Governance observations
while respecting confidentiality requirements.

Policy Statement
Consistent case management promotes transparency, accountability, procedural fairness, and organizational stability.

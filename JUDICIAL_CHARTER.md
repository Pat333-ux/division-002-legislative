Judicial Charter
Division 003 Judicial
Article I - Establishment
The Judicial Division is established as an independent governance review body within the New World Order DAO organizational framework.

Article II - Purpose
The Judicial Division exists to provide impartial review of governance actions, policy interpretation, compliance oversight, administrative appeals, and organizational disputes.

Article III - Jurisdiction
The Judicial Division may review:

Executive Directives
Legislative Policies
Administrative Decisions
Compliance Matters
Governance Disputes
Appeals Submitted By Members
Article IV - Powers
The Judicial Division may:

Publish Judicial Opinions
Issue Governance Rulings
Conduct Compliance Reviews
Review Policy Consistency
Recommend Corrective Actions
Maintain Governance Records
Article V - Transparency
All final rulings, opinions, and compliance determinations shall be documented and published within the division repository unless restricted by applicable law or approved confidentiality requirements.

Article VI - Independence
The Judicial Division shall maintain independent review authority and operate according to published governance standards and documented procedural requirements.

Article VII - Records
The Judicial Division shall maintain:

Rulings
Opinions
Appeals
Compliance Reports
Governance Reviews
Founding Declaration
This Charter serves as the foundational authority document for Division 003 Judicial within the New World Order DAO ecosystem.

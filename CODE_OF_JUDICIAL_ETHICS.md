Code of Judicial Ethics
Division 003 Judicial
Purpose
This Code of Judicial Ethics establishes the standards of conduct, integrity, impartiality, and accountability required within the Judicial Division.

Canon I - Integrity
Members of the Judicial Division shall uphold the integrity of the governance system and act in a manner that promotes confidence in judicial review processes.

Required Conduct
Honesty
Professionalism
Accountability
Accuracy
Transparency
Canon II - Impartiality
Judicial reviews shall be conducted fairly and without favoritism toward any member, division, officer, or governing authority.

Judicial decisions shall be based upon:

Documented evidence
Published policies
Governance standards
Established procedures
Canon III - Independence
The Judicial Division shall exercise independent judgment and shall not permit improper influence on rulings, opinions, or compliance findings.

Canon IV - Transparency
Whenever appropriate, judicial findings shall be documented and published for public review.

Transparency includes:

Case records
Published rulings
Formal opinions
Compliance findings
Canon V - Confidentiality
Confidential information obtained during investigations shall be protected and used solely for legitimate review purposes.

Canon VI - Conflicts of Interest
Participants in judicial reviews shall disclose any actual or perceived conflict of interest.

Examples include:

Financial interests
Personal relationships
Organizational interests
Prior involvement in the matter under review
Canon VII - Accountability
The Judicial Division shall maintain accurate records and be accountable for all official decisions and published findings.

Canon VIII - Due Process
Individuals and divisions subject to review shall receive:

Notice of review
Opportunity to respond
Access to findings when appropriate
Fair consideration of evidence
Canon IX - Organizational Wellbeing
Judicial actions shall support long-term stability, fairness, transparency, accountability, and organizational wellbeing.

Canon X - Continuous Improvement
The Judicial Division shall periodically review procedures, ethics standards, and operational practices to improve governance effectiveness.

Ethical Commitment
All Judicial Division activities shall be guided by integrity, fairness, independence, transparency, accountability, and respect for due process.

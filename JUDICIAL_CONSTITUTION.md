Judicial Constitution
Division 003 Judicial
Preamble
This Constitution establishes the governing principles, authority, responsibilities, and procedural framework of the Judicial Division within the New World Order DAO.

Article I - Judicial Authority
The Judicial Division shall serve as the independent body responsible for governance review, policy interpretation, compliance oversight, dispute resolution, and appeal adjudication.

Article II - Jurisdiction
The Judicial Division shall possess authority to review:

Executive actions
Legislative enactments
Administrative decisions
Organizational disputes
Compliance matters
Governance interpretations
Article III - Due Process
All appeals, disputes, and reviews shall be documented and evaluated according to published standards and available evidence.

Article IV - Transparency
Judicial findings shall be preserved in permanent records and published whenever appropriate to promote accountability and organizational learning.

Article V - Equal Review
All matters submitted to the Judicial Division shall receive impartial review without favoritism toward any office, division, member, or governing authority.

Article VI - Compliance Responsibility
The Judicial Division shall monitor alignment between adopted governance standards and organizational operations.

Article VII - Judicial Opinions
Published opinions shall establish guidance for future interpretations and governance reviews.

Article VIII - Appeals
Members and divisions may submit appeals regarding administrative, legislative, or executive decisions.

Article IX - Preservation of Records
The Judicial Division shall maintain an archive of:

Appeals
Opinions
Compliance Reviews
Governance Findings
Judicial Rulings
Article X - Amendments
This Constitution may be amended through approved governance procedures and recorded revision history.

Foundational Principle
Justice, transparency, accountability, due process, and organizational wellbeing shall guide all actions of the Judicial Division.

# JUDICIAL PRECEDENT POLICY

## 1. PURPOSE
The Judicial Precedent Policy establishes the framework by which decisions, rulings, and opinions issued by Division 003 Judicial create binding authority, persuasive guidance, and operational doctrine across the New World Order DAO LLC ecosystem and BEAST System 3.0.

## 2. JURISDICTION & SCOPE
This policy applies to all executive decisions, legislative acts, smart contract governance parameters, and administrative actions subject to Division 003 Judicial review.

## 3. DOCTRINE OF PRECEDENT (*Stare Decisis*)
1. **Binding Precedent:** Formal Rulings issued by Division 003 Judicial carry binding operational and legal authority across all DAO divisions and smart contract parameters.
2. **Persuasive Precedent:** Judicial Opinions and Compliance Advisory Reports provide interpretive guidance and persuasive authority for future governance reviews.
3. **Overruling & Reconsideration:** Prior rulings may only be modified or overruled through a formal Judicial Review Proceeding with documented legal or operational rationale.

## 4. PRECEDENT HIERARCHY
1. Founding Rulings and Constitutional Opinions
2. En Banc Judicial Rulings
3. Panel Review Decisions
4. Advisory Opinions & Compliance Guidelines

## 5. REPOSITORY INTEGRATION
All precedents must be indexed within `JUDICIAL_CASE_MANAGEMENT_POLICY.md` and archived under `JUDICIAL_RECORDS_RETENTION_POLICY.md` to guarantee immutable record keeping and operational continuity.

Judicial Records Retention Policy
Division 003 Judicial
Purpose
This policy establishes the standards for maintaining, preserving, protecting, archiving, and disposing of official Judicial Division records.

Section 1 - Policy Statement
The Judicial Division shall maintain complete and accurate records to ensure transparency, accountability, historical preservation, and governance continuity.

Section 2 - Records Covered
This policy applies to:

Judicial Rulings
Judicial Opinions
Appeal Records
Compliance Reviews
Investigation Reports
Governance Reviews
Administrative Records
Policy Interpretations
Case Management Records
Section 3 - Record Classifications
Permanent Records
The following records shall be permanently retained:

Judicial Constitutions
Judicial Charters
Final Rulings
Judicial Opinions
Compliance Reports
Governance Determinations
Operational Records
Records supporting daily operations shall be maintained according to operational requirements.

Archived Records
Closed records shall be transferred to archives while maintaining accessibility for authorized review.

Section 4 - Record Integrity
Records shall:

Remain complete
Remain accurate
Preserve version history
Maintain authorship history
Prevent unauthorized alteration
Section 5 - Digital Preservation
Official records may be preserved using:

Git Repository History
Cloud Storage Systems
Governance Archives
Digital Backups
Document Repositories
Section 6 - Archive Standards
Archives shall maintain:

Original Record Date
Record Number
Classification
Status
Source Division
Related Case References
Section 7 - Public Access
Public records may be published when appropriate to support transparency and institutional accountability.

Restrictions may apply to:

Sensitive Information
Confidential Matters
Protected Records
Active Investigations
Section 8 - Version Control
Document revisions shall include:

Version Number
Revision Date
Change Description
Author Identification
Previous versions shall remain preserved when feasible.

Section 9 - Disaster Recovery
The Judicial Division shall maintain reasonable procedures for recovery of critical records following system interruption, data loss, or operational disruption.

Section 10 - Historical Preservation
Records determined to have significant governance value shall be preserved as part of the institutional history of the New World Order DAO.

Section 11 - Annual Review
Records management practices may be reviewed annually to improve preservation, security, accessibility, and governance continuity.

Policy Objective
The purpose of records retention is to preserve institutional knowledge, support accountability, strengthen governance continuity, and maintain trust in judicial operations.
